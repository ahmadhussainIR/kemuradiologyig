KemRad programs

Designed for students at every stage.

KemRad programs are built to make radiology more approachable through case-based learning, mentor conversations, academic support, and early clinical exposure.

What KemRad focuses on

Clear pathways into an image-guided field.

01

Imaging fundamentals

Build comfort with anatomy, modality choice, common findings, and the clinical reasoning behind imaging requests.

02

Case-based learning

Practice pattern recognition through bite-sized cases, discussions, and student teaching sessions.

03

Mentorship and careers

Connect students with conversations about radiology training, daily practice, electives, and application planning.

04

Research readiness

Help students move from curiosity to contribution through project ideas, abstracts, literature reviews, and collaboration.

Programming

Built for practical radiology exposure.

Activities can shift with the academic calendar, student interest, and physician availability while keeping the focus on practical radiology exposure.

Learn

Radiology essentials

Introductory sessions on modality basics, anatomy review, and clinical imaging questions.

Discuss

Case rounds

Short, interactive case discussions that make image interpretation less intimidating.

Meet

Mentor conversations

Panels and Q&A sessions with doctors, residents, alumni, and students pursuing radiology.

Build

Research and presentation support

Guidance for students interested in papers, posters, audits, and academic radiology.
